## How to Enter Data

### Adding Stories
1. Go to `/share-voice` on the website
2. Complete the story submission form
3. Stories appear automatically in the report once approved

### Updating Statistics
1. Contact Ben Knight or Communications team
2. Provide data in this checklist format
3. Data will be entered into the system

### Adding Photos
1. Email to communications team
2. Include: date, event, people in photo, permissions
3. Photos will be tagged and added to gallery
