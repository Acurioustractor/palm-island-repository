## Headline

Ben Knight is the **producer** of the FY24-25 annual report and the **architect** of the platform PICC uses to make its annual reports an always-on system instead of a yearly scramble.

He is not staff. He is not Board. He is the trusted external collaborator who built the Empathy Ledger and is now applying it to PICC. Rachel and Narelle treat him as a peer, not a vendor.

PICC is one of four anchor communities in his STAY work. PICC is the one closest to him operationally — most code, most content, most live work, most photos.

## How his mind works on PICC specifically

- **Deeply visual.** Photos before words. He scans an asset library before he scans a brief.
- **Editor by instinct.** He will rewrite a CEO message until it sounds like Rachel reading it aloud, not until it satisfies a template.
- **Architectural about the system.** He doesn't just want the FY24-25 report — he wants the *system that produces FY24-25 and FY25-26 and FY26-27 with progressively less effort each time*.
- **Patient with cultural protocol.** He will hold a story rather than rush it. He will pause printing rather than override the Elders Group.

## What triggers him on PICC

- **AI-vocab anywhere in PICC copy.** Read the SOUL.md. He will rewrite the sentence and remind you.
- **Generic ATSICCO copy** that could fit any organisation. PICC is specific. The copy must be specific.
- **Numbers without sources.** Every figure has a provenance: Mark, audit, operations team, public record.
- **Generated images of Indigenous people.** Brand rule. Hard line. Real photos for real people. Generated imagery is for atmosphere, motif, and place.
- **Cultural language used decoratively.** *Bwgcolman* is not a brand element. *Many tribes, one people* is a vow, not a tagline.
- **Pretending the AI wrote this.** The AI assisted. Named editors are accountable.
- **Forgetting Narelle's corrections.** She walked through the service list once. The list now is the list. Don't drift.

- **Drafts go to Obsidian first.** The master report file is `.md` until layout begins.
