## Approach: THE LAUNCHPAD

Use the 20-year moment as a **deadline and amplifier for infrastructure**. The celebration is a launch event for the new PICC brand + website + Empathy Ledger. Every story captured, every service linked, every Elder voice recorded is content for the launch AND fuel for the next 20 years.

- Community ceremony sits at the centre
- Rebrand and platform launch wrap around it
- Bwgcolman Way is the concrete proof point
- Funder/sector attention lives in the same week but a different room
- The Empathy Ledger is invisible plumbing, never the hero

## Mandatory scope (must ship for the moment to land)

### M1. A specific date
Without a date, nothing has a deadline and everything drifts. The celebration needs a week, ideally a day.

### M2. The "next 20 years" vision document — Rachel-led
The single biggest gap in the current plan. The email talks about celebration and rebrand but never names the forward payload. Without this, The Launchpad has nothing to launch. Rachel writes this. Ben helps structure it.

**First draft sketch (for Rachel to rewrite):**
> "PICC's next 20 years: community-controlled infrastructure for every Palm Islander. Aged care on Palm by 2028. Delegated Authority expanded into health and justice by 2030. A permanent archive of community voice that grows stronger every year. A rebrand that belongs to community, not to outsiders. Every Palm Island story captured, consented, and sovereign by 2045."

### M3. The Bwgcolman Way case study
1,500-word policy-grade case study of Bwgcolman Way as a world-first Indigenous-led delegated authority for child safety. The anchor of the 20-year story. The one thing every funder, minister, and peer org remembers.

### M4. The "Ben + Nic behind the curtain" commitment
Mandatory principle, not an assumption. Every public-facing asset is in community voice. Every stage moment is PICC, not consultants. If anyone ever asks who built the platform, the answer is "PICC and community, with technical help from Ben and Nic". Never the other way around.

### M5. Community and Elder sign-off workflow running at scale
Start a weekly Elder review cycle NOW. 669 voices are pending approval. Nine months of weekly review sessions at 10 voices each gets 360 approved. Start immediately or the content cliff kills the launch.

### M6. Core website + rebrand live before the celebration
New picc.studio, new brand, Hull River entry point, year-by-year timeline, voices embedded. Ready for outsiders by the launch week.

### M7. One-day community ceremony on Palm, Elder-led
Not Ben's event. Not the agency's event. Community's event, on Palm, with Elders leading.

### M8. Separate funder/sector briefing in the same week, different room
Half-day closed-doors session. Rachel chairs. Bwgcolman Way case study. Next-20 vision announcement. Peer CEO panel.

## Accepted expansions (opted in during review)

### E1. "20 Voices for 20 Years" capture sprint
60 voices captured and approved before the celebration. One Elder, one staff, one young person per year 2005–2025. Each with photo, short video, written quote, tied to a specific year. Starts this month. Rachel and Narelle prioritise the must-capture list.

**Why time-sensitive:** Some Elders may not be capturable in 12 months. Waiting costs irreplaceable content.

### E2. Physical archive — book + installation (smaller scale)
- **Book:** 80-page hardcover, ~100 copies. Community-authored, voices from the Ledger, Bwgcolman Way as feature chapter. Becomes the gift Rachel hands every Elder and partner.
- **Installation:** Simple year-by-year photo wall at The Centre. Permanent.
- **Budget:** ~$30-45k combined
- **Lead time:** ~3 months

### E3. Full funder/sector briefing session
Half-day event during celebration week. 30-50 invited guests: Philanthropy Australia, NIAA, federal ministers' offices, 3-5 peer First Nations CEOs, 2-3 major philanthropic funders. Rachel chairs. Invitations out 4-6 months ahead.

**Strategic purpose:** Convert peak attention into policy leverage and funding commitments for the next 20 years.

### E4. Year-by-year 2005→2025 interactive timeline page (stated mandatory given E1)
The home for the 20 voices on the new website. Year by year, each year with: voices, photos, annual report excerpts, key stats, milestones, Hull River threads. Lives as the primary entry point to picc.studio.

### E5. Hull River long-form anchor piece
2,000-3,000 word written version plus 20-minute audio documentary using Elder voices from the on-Country trips. Lives on the new website as THE entry point to PICC's story. Frame: *"Before PICC, there was Hull River. Before Hull River, there was Country."* Full Elder cultural sign-off required.
