# APPENDIX — Facilitator's notes

## How to run this session

**If Rachel is energised and talks a lot:**

- Let her. The 20-year vision section (Slides 10–12) is where you want her to dominate.
- Use the facilitator's discipline: every time she gives you a direction on one of the seven decisions, write it down and say "captured."
- Don't let her leave the room without the sentence in Slide 12 being drafted.

**If Rachel is tired or distracted:**

- Skip Slide 4 (foundation stats) and Slide 8 (Narelle quotes) — they're helpful but not critical.
- Collapse Section 3 (data plan) into "I'll send you the list of owners we need confirmed, reply by end of week."
- The non-negotiables are: Slide 5 (service decisions), Slide 12 (the sentence), Slide 13 (the seven decisions).

**If Rachel challenges the frame:**

- Don't defend the Launchpad plan. It's a draft. Let her rewrite it.
- The goal is not that she accepts your plan — the goal is that she owns the next-20 vision. If she tears up your plan and puts a better one in its place, you've won.

## What to bring into the room

- Printed copy of the updated services review (post-Narelle)
- Printed copy of the Launchpad plan
- Printed copy of this deck (talking points included)
- Whiteboard or shared doc for Slide 12 live drafting
- A pen. Write down directions as she gives them.

## What NOT to bring

- The 10x national movement idea (Approach C from the original plan) — not today
- Multi-tenant platform expansion — not today
- Any consulting scope-of-work conversation — this is strategy, not contracting
- A defensive attitude about anything you've already built

## After the session (within 48 hours)

1. Write up the decisions in plain language — what Rachel said yes to, what's open, what she rejected
2. Transcribe the draft vision sentence from Slide 12 exactly as she said it
3. Update the Launchpad plan with the new vision sentence in M2
4. Send Rachel a one-page summary for confirmation
5. Only after her confirmation: share the updates with Narelle and anyone else on the core team
