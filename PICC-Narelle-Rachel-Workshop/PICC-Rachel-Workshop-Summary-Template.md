# Rachel Workshop — 48-Hour Summary

**Template for Ben to fill in within 48 hours of the Rachel workshop.**

This is the single document Rachel gets back after the session. Its job is to make sure nothing gets lost in translation, nothing gets revised in her memory, and everything she said lands on paper exactly as she said it. It doubles as the input that updates the Launchpad plan and unlocks the next phase of work.

**Send as:** email with this document pasted in-line, OR Google Doc link with commenter access, OR both.
**Cadence:** within 48 hours of the workshop. No later. The longer you wait the more her memory drifts.
**Tone:** her voice, not yours. Your job is scribe, not interpreter.

---

## How to use this template

1. Fill in every `[...]` placeholder with what actually happened in the room.
2. If she didn't decide something, write "OPEN — to be resolved in the follow-up call on [date]." Don't fudge it.
3. If she changed her mind mid-session, capture the final position only, but note that it shifted.
4. Read the whole thing back to yourself before sending. If any sentence sounds like Ben wrote it, rewrite it to sound like Rachel said it.
5. Delete this "How to use" section before sending to Rachel.
