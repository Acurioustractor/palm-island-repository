## The six documents in this folder

### 1. [[PICC-Services-Projects-Review-for-Narelle]]

The operational truth-check. Every PICC service (28) and project (6) as it sits in the Empathy Ledger today, with space under each one for Narelle's reflections, voices to capture, and 24/25 data gaps.

**Use:** Share with Narelle before the call. Commenter access in Google Docs. She marks it up.

---

### 2. [[PICC-Narelle-Interview-Framework]]

The structured conversation. One 90–120 minute session that feeds four outputs at once:

- 24/25 Annual Report
- Always-on Empathy Ledger profile
- 20-Year Celebration and next-20 vision
- Bwgcolman Way case study

Every question tagged with which output it feeds, so Narelle never has to repeat herself.

**Use:** Ben runs this in the video call with Narelle. Print a copy. Record the call with consent.

---

### 3. [[PICC-Narelle-Email-Draft]]

Three versions of the email to Narelle asking for the video walkthrough — short (recommended), longer, minimal. Plus a pre-send checklist and a post-call workflow.

**Use:** Pick the version that fits Narelle's inbox. Upload the two Narelle-facing docs to Google Docs first, paste the links in, hit send.

---

### 4. [[PICC-20-Year-Launchpad-Plan]]

The strategic baseline. The Launchpad approach — using the 20-year moment as a deadline and amplifier for the new PICC brand, website, and Empathy Ledger. Includes mandatory scope (M1–M8), accepted expansions (E1–E5), and the nine things Ben needs from Rachel in the workshop.

**Use:** Reference doc for the Rachel workshop. Gets updated with Rachel's decisions after the session.

---

### 5. [[PICC-Rachel-Workshop-Slides]]

The 90-minute session with Rachel. Sixteen slides across six sections. Each slide has **WHAT RACHEL SEES** (slide content) and **WHAT BEN SAYS** (talking points). Built to produce three things: a date, a sentence, and seven decisions.

**Use:** Run this after the Narelle interview is complete. Can be presented as slides or run as a structured conversation with the markdown open on your laptop.

---

### 6. [[PICC-Rachel-Workshop-Summary-Template]]

The 48-hour post-workshop summary. A fill-in-the-blanks template Ben uses to write up what Rachel decided within 48 hours of the session — her exact sentence, the seven decisions, the service truth, what's still open, and what happens next. Sent back to Rachel for confirmation. Her confirmation is the trigger that unlocks the next phase of work.

**Use:** Open this within 48 hours of the Rachel workshop. Fill every `[...]` placeholder with what actually happened in the room. Delete the "How to use" and "Appendix" sections. Send to Rachel.
