### 5. Health and Wellbeing Outcomes

#### 5.1 Key Health Challenges

**Life Expectancy Gap**
- National Indigenous average: 71.9 years (vs 83.2 non-Indigenous)
- Palm Island likely at or below average
- ~11 year gap

**Chronic Disease Burden**
| Condition | Palm Island Estimate | National Average |
|-----------|---------------------|------------------|
| Type 2 Diabetes | 20-30% adults | 5.3% |
| Smoking Rate | 40-50% | ~10% |
| Cardiovascular Disease | High | Moderate |
| Kidney Disease | High (linked to diabetes) | Low |

**Mental Health**
- High rates of depression, anxiety, PTSD
- Intergenerational trauma impacts
- Suicide risk (especially young men)

**Social Determinants**
- Overcrowding: up to 15-20 people per house
- Housing shortfall: 100+ households
- Food insecurity due to remote location costs

#### 5.2 Health Services - Bwgcolman Healing Service

**Comprehensive Primary Care:**
- General practice and nursing
- Chronic disease management
- Maternal and child health
- Mental health support
- Health promotion and prevention

**Culturally Safe Care:**
- Aboriginal Health Workers
- Cultural protocols respected
- Trauma-informed practice
- Holistic approach integrating Western and cultural practices

### 6. Healthy Eating Initiatives

#### 6.1 Challenges
- High cost of food on island (freight costs)
- Limited fresh fruit and vegetables availability
- Poverty limiting healthy food purchases
- Limited cooking facilities in overcrowded homes
- Preference/availability of processed foods

### 8. Key Innovations and Outcomes

#### 8.1 Integrated Service Delivery Model

#### 8.2 Digital Service Centre (June 2023)
