## §4 — OpenAI image prompt library (Pencil-ready)

Comprehensive prompt library for OpenAI's image model (e.g. DALL·E 4 or successor). Prompts are written in three tiers:

- **T1 — Cover & full-page hero washes** (run wide, atmospheric)
- **T2 — Section dividers & decorative elements** (single-purpose, brand-aligned)
- **T3 — Service-specific motifs** (where photos are missing)

Every prompt uses a shared **Style Stem** to keep visual language consistent across the report.

### A. Style Stem (paste at the END of every prompt)

```
Style: contemporary Indigenous-inspired illustration in the PICC Saltwater & Earth palette — deep ocean blue (#0B4F6C), ochre gold (#C8963E), warm earth (#2D2319), sand cream (#FEF3C7), turtle red (#8B1A1A), mangrove green (#15803D). Painterly digital, suggestive not literal. Restrained. Dignified. No corporate gloss. No clip-art. Subtle Indigenous-art-influenced dot work permissible. Composition: centred, room for type overlay. Format: A4 portrait, 300dpi, full-bleed safe. No text. No logos. No watermarks. No literal figures unless the prompt requests them — and never photo-realistic figures.
```

### B. Hard rules

- **No photo-realistic Indigenous people generated.** Ever. The brand rule is real photos for real people. Generated imagery is for atmosphere, motif, and place — never for human faces.
- **No fake objects of cultural significance.** Generated dot work is ornamental only. It must not pretend to be a specific cultural artefact, design, or story.
- **Do not generate cultural ceremony.** The line between "atmospheric" and "appropriative" is real. If a prompt would produce an image of dance, smoking ceremony, men's or women's business, song lines — do not run it.
- **Generate three variants per prompt minimum.** Pick with Rachel.
- **All generated images live in `web-platform/public/report-assets/generated/`** with prompt + date + seed in filename for re-runnability.
