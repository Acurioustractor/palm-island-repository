## A message from our Chair

PICC belongs to the community.

That sentence is the whole of it, and the whole of why this year matters. In 2021 the community took ownership of this organisation in full. In 2024-25 the community took ownership of decisions about our own children. Bwgcolman Way is now law on Palm Island — the first time in Queensland that an Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Community Controlled Organisation has been granted Delegated Authority for child protection.

Distant governments telling Palm Island what to do and how to do it has never worked and will never work. This year is the proof.

### What the Board did this year

The Board's job is to keep PICC sharp, accountable, and ours. This year that meant:

- **Standing behind Bwgcolman Way as it went live** — and standing behind Rachel and her team as the work of it began.
- **Holding the line on local employment.** Three out of four PICC staff are Indigenous. Seven out of ten live on Palm. We do not move on these numbers. They are the point.
- **Backing two new launches** — the First 1,000 Days Program in April 2024, and the Blue Card Liaison Service pilot in July 2024. Both are about removing the barriers that have stopped Palm Island families from being able to look after Palm Island children.
- **Approving the PIC Leadership Program** that begins next month. Designed by our own people, for our own people. The next twenty years of PICC leadership starts here.
- **Listening to our Elders Group** — who sit at the heart of how this organisation makes decisions, not at the edge of it.

### What I see in the community

The positive changes we strive for are becoming evident — particularly among our young people, who are beginning to feel a sense of optimism about the future of Palm Island. I see it at the Daycare. I see it in the boys playing organised footy for the first time. I see it in the women coming back to Country with our Elders. I see it in the staff who, ten years ago, had never worked a day in a service like ours, and now run them.

### Where we go from here

In October 2027, PICC turns twenty.

We will mark that milestone honestly. Twenty years of work. Twenty years of community control fought for and earned. And twenty more ahead — Aged Care on Palm Island so our Elders never have to leave Country. Bwgcolman Way expanded into health and justice. A community-owned story archive that holds our voices, on our terms, for our grandchildren.

I want to thank the Board, the Elders Group, Rachel and the staff, and every Palm Islander who has ever picked up the phone, walked through the door, or asked a hard question of this organisation. PICC is yours. It only works because you keep it that way.

— **Luella Bligh**, Chair
