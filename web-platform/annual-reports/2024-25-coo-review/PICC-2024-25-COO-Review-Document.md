# 0. Fact-check flags to resolve before publication ⚠️

### 1. Blue Card Liaison Service

**Category:** community · **Status:** pilot (launched July 2024) · **Partner:** Blue Card Services (Qld Govt) · **Included in 2024-25 report:** yes

**Current platform description:**
> Walk-in Blue Card liaison service helping community members navigate the Working with Children Check process. Operated by a trained Blue Card Liaison Officer who translates complex legislation into accessible guidance, supports applicants through the extensive screening process, and coordinates virtual assessments with Blue Card Services officers. Launched as a pilot in July 2024, the service averages around 20 positive notices per month — giving community members access to employment, kinship care, and volunteer roles working with children. A critical barrier-reduction service, particularly for those previously deterred by the eligibility assessment process.

**Key facts:** Walk-in + virtual assessments · ~20 positive notices/month · 21 notices Jan 2025 · estimated ~100/year

### 11. Bwgcolman Way ⭐ anchor story for 2024-25

**Category:** family · **Staff:** 10 · **Funding:** $107.8M over 4 years 2023-2028 (**statewide**, not Palm-only — §0 flag #2) · **Legislation:** Child Protection Act 1999 (Qld), Part 2A, s.148BA · **Blueprint:** "Reclaiming our Storyline" (April 2023, QATSICPP/DCSSDS)

**Current platform description:**
> Historic milestone service — PICC became the first Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Community Controlled Organisation in Queensland granted Delegated Authority for child protection under the Child Protection Act 1999, s.148BA. Decisions about Palm Island children are now made by Palm Island people through the Bwgcolman Way blueprint "Reclaiming our Storyline" (April 2023). Funded $107.8M over four years (2023–2028) by the Queensland Government, this service represents a fundamental shift in how child protection operates in community — moving from government-led intervention to community-led care and decision-making.

**Linked evidence:**
- Transcripts: `picc-vault/transcripts/bwgcolman-way-community-control-for-our-children.md` (comprehensive overview) · `picc-vault/transcripts/empowering-palm-island-a-community-control-success-story.md` · `picc-vault/transcripts/picc-shares-our-story-at-national-conference.md`
- Key quotes:
  - *"Bwgcolman means many tribes, one people."* — foundational principle
  - *"Our vision is that all Manbarra and Bwgcolman children are safe and cared for by family, nurtured by strong and enduring connections to their community, culture and Country."*
  - *"Palm Island mob leading and creating change for Palm Island children, young people and families."*
  - *"This change has been decades in the making."*

**External context (critical — see §0 flags #1 and #2):**
- Legislation: Part 2A of the Child Protection Act 1999 (Qld) — "Prescribed delegates for Aboriginal or Torres Strait Islander children". s.148BA defines the ATSI entity; under Part 2A the Chief Executive delegates statutory functions to the CEO of the ATSICCO ("prescribed delegate") — the Chief Executive retains legal responsibility.
- Scale (public record): **"over 100 children and young people"** under DA as of 2024-25 (QATSICPP/DCSSDS public material). PICC's 439 figure is from an internal briefing — cite the source or revise.
- Blueprint: "Reclaiming Our Storyline — Transforming systems and practice by making decisions in our way" (QATSICPP + DCSSDS, published Sep 2023; full PDF on QATSICPP site).
- Companion document: "DA Story of Change" (QATSICPP CoE, Feb 2025) — progress narrative.
- PICC is named as a **Promising Practice case study** on the QATSICPP Centre of Excellence site.
- Sources:
  - legislation.qld.gov.au/view/whole/html/current/act-1999-010
  - families.qld.gov.au/our-work/child-safety/aboriginal-torres-strait-islander-families/delegated-authority
  - qatsicpp.com.au/delegated-authority-frequently-asked-questions
  - coe.qatsicpp.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/DA_Story-of-Change.pdf
  - qatsicpp.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Annual-report-24-25-v1-Web.pdf

**Cross-service connections:** Safe House (placement decisions) · Safe Haven · Blue Card Liaison · Family Care Service · Family Participation Program · Children and Family Centre
