## Services — final 24 (was 30; six consolidated/removed)

Grouped as: **Children & Families · Health & Wellbeing · Justice · Youth · Economic · Education · Community.**
Re-grouping recommendation for Pencil: lead with Children & Families (Bwgcolman Way anchor), then Health & Wellbeing, then everything else.

### Children & Families (7)

1. **Bwgcolman Way** — *anchor story.* First ATSICCO in **Queensland** granted Delegated Authority for child protection under the Child Protection Act 1999, Part 2A. Decisions about Palm Island children made by Palm Island people, guided by the *Reclaiming our Storyline* blueprint. Funded as part of Queensland's $107.8M four-year statewide DA commitment (PICC is one of the first ATSICCOs to go live; the $107.8M is statewide, not PICC-only).

2. **CFC Early Childhood Service** — quality early childhood education and care from the Children and Family Centre. Rebuilt by community after the 2024 floods. Presented "The Storyline of the Palm Island CFC" at SNAICC '23 in Darwin.

3. **First 1,000 Days Program** — launched April 2024. Child Health Nurse + Aboriginal Health Worker + GP, working with Bwgcolman Healing Service to wrap around families from conception to age 2.

4. **Family Care Service** — PICC's kinship care program. Family and cultural support for kinship and home care arrangements. 6,698 placement nights in 2023-24.

5. **Family Participation Program** — supports families to participate in decisions about their children in the child protection system; maintains cultural connections through care.

6. **Family Wellbeing Centre** — comprehensive family support: child safety, DFV response, parenting, family strengthening. ~6–8 staff. ~450 families a year. Co-located with Women's Shelter.

7. **Safe House** — residential out-of-home care for up to 6 children at a time; stays up to 3 months. Family-staffed, on-Country, culturally grounded. 1,439 placement nights in 2023-24. Works with Bwgcolman Way on placement decisions.

### Health & Wellbeing (5)

8. **Bwgcolman Healing Service** — RACGP-accredited (2024–2027) primary health service. 35 staff. 2,283 clients · 17,488 episodes of care (2023-24). Renamed from Primary Health Centre in early 2024 after community consultation. Programs include Integrated Team Care, Communicable Infections, ARF/RHD, Eldercare Connector, Growing Deadly Families, GP After Hours.

9. **Social and Emotional Wellbeing (SEWB)** — culturally appropriate mental health, counselling, crisis intervention; trauma, grief, psychological health.

10. **Women's Healing Service** — supports First Nations women at risk of or involved in the criminal justice system. Operates across Palm Island, Aitkenvale (Townsville), and TWCC. Tailored individual support: child safety advocacy, DFV, AOD, mental health, legal liaison, housing. Programs include the Women's Yarning Circle (with Elders for Change), TARS Health Clinic, dignity partnerships (Thread Together, Share the Dignity, Suits with Sits), and SPER hardship support. Six-week pre-release information program for women returning to remote communities.

11. **Ferdy's Haven** — day-based social and emotional wellbeing service supporting women and men 18+. Originally established in 1993 as a drug and alcohol rehabilitation centre; women's groups since 2014. Recently expanded to include men's groups following male-staff recruitment. Casework, weekly groups (Tuesdays/Wednesdays), and a safe quiet space. Targeted education program for high school girls in partnership with the local school.

12. **Women's Shelter** — emergency accommodation for women and children escaping domestic and family violence. 4 self-contained units (one disability-accessible). 12 staff. Up to 3-week stays. Referrals from police, hospitals, and the DFV service. Remodelled 2024.

### Justice & Safety (3)

13. **Specialist Domestic and Family Violence Service** — specialist DFV support: crisis intervention, safety planning, court support, counselling, prevention education. Houses the **Men's Pathway to Healing** program (men's group / men's business work led by Clay Alfred).

14. **Community Justice Group** — auspiced by PICC since 2008. General Program plus the DFV Enhancement Program. 6 part-time staff. ~120 community members supported each year. Works alongside (not under) the Diversionary Program.

15. **Diversionary Program** — community-based men's and women's programs and outreach (≈95% male clientele). Weekly programs include goal-setting, fishing, bushwalking, gym, men's gathering, haircut program, and cemetery maintenance. Outreach picks up women across the community as well. Positive activities, mentorship, and role-modelling to reduce justice-system contact.

### Youth (2)

16. **Safe Haven** — *not residential.* Youth patrol, night café, school holiday activities, and the tuck shop and breakfast program at Bwgcolman Community School. ~1,187 instances of support across 2023-24. Run by Jeannie.

17. **Youth Services** — works with disengaged young people and those in the justice system. Combines the **Youth Offender Support Service (YOSS)** and the **Indigenous Youth Connection to Culture** program under one team (managed by Dee). 15 staff, ~380 young people a year. Programs around fishing, touch football, sport, and a youth hub in a converted bakery (TVs, couches, PlayStations). Fielded an under-15s team in the Christmas Cup. Connects young people to regional camps like RISE.

### Economic (3)

18. **Digital Service Centre** — opened 2023; first calls October 2023. Sales and customer service for Telstra products to Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander customers, with interpreters in ~50 First Nations languages. 21 Palm Islanders employed (capacity 30). Training pathway: 12-week TAFE course + 5-week Telstra induction + Certificate III in Business. Partners: Telstra, Palm Island Aboriginal Shire Council, State of Queensland, Advance Queensland.

19. **Retail Community Shop** — PICC social enterprise: coffee shop and goods and services, creating local employment. (Not based in the new Palm Island Retail Centre.)

20. **Logistics** — operations across Townsville and Palm Island. Movement of goods, catering equipment, event support, deliveries, and supply chain for PICC programs. Run by Shane; overseen by Mizlam.

### Education & Community (4)

21. **Bwgcolman Education and Training Service (BEAI)** — NIAA-funded education engagement. Four streams: in-school support, community engagement, communications, and rewards & recognition. Holistic pathway from early childhood (CFC) through Bwgcolman Community School to YOSS and PCYC. Engagement officers float between classrooms. Friday work includes boarding-school enrolment support (with MARUP) and community surveys.

22. **Blue Card Liaison Service** — pilot launched July 2024 in partnership with Blue Card Services (Qld Govt). Walk-in and virtual support to navigate the Working with Children Check. ~20 positive notices per month. Funding cliff 30 June 2026.

23. **Community Hub** — central community gathering and coordination point on Palm Island; coordinates major community events, food hampers, and supports peak events across PICC services. Supported by the **RJED** (Remote Jobs) program. Pilot mowing program in partnership with the Youth Justice team — young people on probation learn yard maintenance while supporting Elders.

24. **NDIS Services** — National Disability Insurance Scheme support coordination, community connection, and access services, **including home care packages and elder care support**. Tripled in size in the last year. ~15 staff across Townsville and Palm. Aitkenvale office opened February 2024.
