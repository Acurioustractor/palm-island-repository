# PICC 20-Year Brand Vision

**Version 1.0** | April 2026
**Horizon:** October 2027 — 20 years of Palm Island Community Company
**Sibling to:** `PICC-BRAND-STYLE-GUIDE.md` v2.0 (the brand foundation) and `ANNUAL-REPORT-EDITORIAL.md` v1.0 (editorial application)

---

## Thesis

The 20-year celebration is **not** a re-brand. It is a deliberate evolution — the brand matures into itself. The turtle, the concentric dots, saltwater country, and Bwgcolman voice stay exactly where they are. What changes is the *vocabulary of expression* built on top of them: a denser graphic language, a deeper archival use of photography, and a commemorative typography treatment reserved for the 20-year arc.

By October 2027 the system must feel like the same brand, older, quieter, and more certain of itself.

## What evolves

### 1. The Twenty motif

A new primary decorative element joins the seven existing motifs. It is the only truly new graphic in the system.

**Form:** twenty concentric dots arranged as two tightening spiral arcs — ten dots on each arm, converging on a shared centre. Reads as motion and as completeness.

**Derivation:** the outer rings of the logo unrolled into a spiral. Not a new shape; a re-phrasing of an existing one.

**Usage, exclusive:**
- Cover of the 20-year publication
- Opening frame of the Oct 2027 exhibition
- Anniversary page headers across the commemorative digital site
- Single ceremonial use on the annual report 2026-27 cover page

**Not usage:** anywhere in the standard system after Oct 2027. The motif retires with the celebration.

**Colours:** gradient from Turtle Red `#8B1A1A` (innermost dot) through Ochre `#C8963E` (midpoint) to Star Gold `#F5A623` (outer). The gradient tracks the arc, not a fill.

### 2. Commemorative typography

Caveat earns one additional time-limited exception — mirroring the structure of the Elder pull-quote exception in `ANNUAL-REPORT-EDITORIAL.md` §4.

| Context | Treatment |
|---|---|
| The phrase "20 years" (numeric or spelled) | Caveat 72pt Bold, Ocean → Star Gold vertical gradient, letter-spacing −2 |
| The phrase "Year 20" | Caveat 44pt Bold, Turtle Red |
| Decade markers ("Year 2008", "Year 2017") in the timeline | Caveat 24pt Regular, Ochre |

Every other headline in the 20-year system uses the standard H1/H2 scale from the brand guide. Caveat is not scattered; it is earned at three specific moments.

### 3. Palette modes — celebration overlay

The Saltwater & Earth palette stays as the base. Three **mode overlays** are added — each is a restricted subset that signals which commemorative surface you are looking at.

| Mode | Dominant | Accent | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sunrise** (horizon, future) | Ochre `#C8963E` | Star Gold `#F5A623` | Exhibition "next" sections, 2027-onward narrative |
| **Saltwater Deep** (archive, memory) | Ocean `#0B4F6C` | Midnight `#1A1A2E` | Timeline pages, archival photos, founding-era content |
| **Hearth** (community, presence) | Turtle Red `#8B1A1A` | Sand `#FEF3C7` | Community portrait sections, Elder voices, Bwgcolman Way spreads |

Within a single spread, one mode dominates. Rule 6 of `ANNUAL-REPORT-EDITORIAL.md` (two service colours + Ocean, max) still applies — the modes are curatorial metadata, not a license to layer more colours.

### 4. Photography language — the archive as anchor

The 20-year system leans harder on photography than the annual report does. Three specific practices:

- **Before / After pairings.** Two photos, same place, years apart. 2009 and 2027. Captioned with year, location, source. Sixty of these are the spine of the 20-year publication.
- **Uncropped Elder portraits.** Where consent allows. Large, quiet, no overlay text. Inter caption below in Driftwood. The Elder holds the page.
- **Country without people.** Every chapter opens on a Palm Island landscape without a human figure. Reef, mangrove, horizon, Hull River. The land as unchanging witness.

Photo treatment modes (Rule 1 of the editorial) still apply. No new mode is added.

### 5. Motion and digital

For the commemorative digital site (anniversary.palmislandcc.com.au or equivalent):

| Interaction | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Page enter | Twenty dots animate in, one per 50ms, then settle. Once per visit; stored in localStorage. |
| Timeline scroll | Year markers pulse briefly on enter (300ms, Ochre). `prefers-reduced-motion` honoured — replaced by a static border highlight. |
| Elder quote reveal | Cross-fade only, 400ms. No slide, no zoom. Voice deserves stillness. |

Motion is a whisper in the 20-year system, not a performance.

### 6. Voice — the commemorative register

The brand guide's tone of voice (§9) is unchanged. One additional editorial register is available for 20-year surfaces only:

**Plain past, plain future.**

> *"In 2008, seven Elders signed a deed. In 2027, we are still here, and so are they."*

No "we are proud to announce." No "it is with great pleasure that we celebrate." Facts, dates, consequences. The longer the view, the quieter the tone.

## The Publication Family — Oct 2027

Five products ship from this system. Each has a defined role.

| Product | Role | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **The 20-Year Book** | Definitive anchor. 160pp hardcover. The archive, in order. | Printed book, 250 copies, community first |
| **Exhibition** | Palm Island + Townsville + travelling | 30 panels, A0, exhibition vinyl + digital kiosk |
| **Anniversary site** | Always-on digital home for the arc | Dedicated subdomain or `/20-years/` on palmislandcc.com.au |
| **Annual Report 2026-27** | The annual report itself, carrying the commemorative cover treatment and a dedicated 8-page insert | Standard AR pipeline, extended |
| **Community Video** | 8–12 minutes. Voices first, land second, statistics third. | MP4 + captioned, distributed via GHL + socials |

These are the only commemorative products. A proposal for a sixth (posters, merch, microsite, NFT, etc.) needs a named reason that is not "because we could."

## Governance

Any 20-year surface requires sign-off from **all three** of:

1. **Rachel Atkinson** (CEO) — strategic and editorial
2. **Elders Council representative** — cultural protocol, photograph consent at the category level
3. **Brand custodian** (current: Ben Knight) — adherence to the system

No single role can green-light a commemorative product alone. This is intentional: the brand belongs to the organisation, the Elders, and the custodians together.

---

## What this document is not

- It is not a re-brand guide. If you find yourself changing the logo, the palette base, or the type pair, stop.
- It is not a marketing campaign. The 20-year system is a commemorative system — it looks inward first.
- It is not licence for more decoration. Read Rule 3 of the editorial again: one motif per spread. The Twenty motif obeys this rule on every surface except the three exclusive uses listed in §1.
- It is not permanent. The Twenty motif and the commemorative Caveat treatment retire in November 2027.
